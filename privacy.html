Privacy Policy — If World Draft Uploader

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Overview

“If World Draft Uploader” (the “App”) is a personal workflow tool operated by the creator of the short-video channel “If World” (the “Operator”). The App sends the Operator's own finished video files to the Operator's own TikTok inbox as unpublished drafts, using TikTok's Content Posting API (Upload) with the video.upload scope. The App does not publish content publicly, and it is not offered as a service to third parties.

2. Information the App accesses

When the Operator authorizes the App through TikTok's official authorization flow, the App receives:

  • An access token and related authorization data issued by TikTok for the Operator's own account, limited to the video.upload scope.
  • Basic account information returned by TikTok for the authorized account (such as the account's user ID and username), used only to confirm that drafts are sent to the correct account.

The App does not access the Operator's TikTok password. The App does not collect information about any other TikTok users.

3. Video files

Video files handled by the App are the Operator's own finished videos. The App transmits a video file to TikTok's servers solely so that it appears in the Operator's TikTok inbox as a draft. The App does not sell, publish, or redistribute video files, and does not use them for advertising or profiling.

4. How information is used

Information accessed by the App is used only to:

  • Authenticate the Operator's own account via TikTok's authorization flow.
  • Upload the Operator's own video files to the Operator's own TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Confirm delivery status of those uploads.

5. Sharing with third parties

The Operator does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Data is transmitted to TikTok only as part of the App's normal operation described above. No analytics or advertising trackers are embedded in the App or its website.

6. Storage and retention

Authorization tokens are stored in access-controlled storage and are retained only while the App is in use. The Operator may revoke the App's access at any time from within TikTok's settings, which invalidates the tokens. Upload status logs, if kept, contain no personal information beyond the Operator's own account identifier and are deleted when no longer needed.

7. Deletion requests and contact

To request deletion of any data associated with this App, or for any privacy-related question, contact:

  • Email: ifworld397@gmail.com

Deletion requests will be handled promptly. Revoking the App's access in TikTok's settings also immediately stops the App from accessing the account.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
